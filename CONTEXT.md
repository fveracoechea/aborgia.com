# Aborgia Insurance

Landing page for an independent insurance broker offering health and life insurance products.

## Language

**Final Expenses**:
A life insurance product designed to cover funeral costs, burial expenses, and other end-of-life financial obligations.
_Avoid_: Burial insurance, funeral insurance

**Life Insurance**:
A policy that pays a death benefit to beneficiaries upon the insured's passing, providing financial protection for dependents.
_Avoid_: Life cover, death benefit plan

**Mortgage Protection**:
A life insurance product specifically structured to pay off a remaining mortgage balance if the insured dies, preventing foreclosure.
_Avoid_: Mortgage insurance, MPI, mortgage life insurance

**Obamacare**:
The Affordable Care Act (ACA) marketplace health insurance plans, subsidized based on income and household size.
_Avoid_: ACA, marketplace plans, exchange plans

**Insurance Offering**:
A specific product category sold by the broker. Each offering is presented as a distinct section on the landing page.
_Avoid_: Product, service, plan
